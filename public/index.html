Objection Quick Reference

When a gym owner pushes back — have the comeback ready in under 5 seconds. Pull this up mid-conversation.

I've never heard of MatMade.
That's exactly why I'm reaching out. MatMade is the largest BJJ gym directory in the world — 8,400+ gyms across all 50 states and 20 countries. Your gym is already listed. We're just making sure you own your page before someone else's info shows up on it.
I already have a website and a Google page. I don't need this.
Those are great for people who already know you exist. MatMade is for people who don't know you yet — someone new to the area, just getting into BJJ, searching for a gym. You're already in the directory. We're just making sure when they find you, they see the best version of your gym.
Why do I have to pay for my own listing?
You don't — claiming is completely free. Takes 2 minutes. The paid tiers are for gym owners who want to stand out from the other gyms in their city. Free gets you in the directory. Pro gets you to the top of it.
How do I know people actually use MatMade?
MatMade has 8,400+ gyms indexed and ranks on Google for "[City] BJJ gym" searches across thousands of cities. The city page for your city already exists and is pulling search traffic. The question isn't whether people are finding it — it's whether your gym looks its best when they do.
I don't have time for this.
Claiming takes 2 minutes. After that we handle everything — we collect the content, format the page, write the story. You just intro us to one student whose life your gym changed. That's it.
We already have plenty of students. We don't need more leads.
That's a good problem to have. This isn't just about leads — it's about legacy. The transformation stories we put on your page and post on the MatMade Instagram are a permanent record of what your gym has built. The best gyms in the country are going to be on here. Where do you want your gym to be in that conversation?
Can I think about it?
Absolutely. Just so you know — we're locking in the city feature spot for [City] this week. There's only one per city and once it's taken it's gone. The listing itself stays free forever, so no rush on that. But if the featured placement is something you want, this week is the window.
The free tier is enough for me.
Totally fine — a lot of gyms start there. The reason most upgrade to Pro is the transformation stories. We collect them, post them on the MatMade Instagram, and they live permanently on your page. Gym owners find that their students share those posts more than anything else they put out. But start with free and see how it feels.
What happens to my content if I cancel?
Your listing stays up. Basic info stays public. The rich content — hero images, videos, stories — those pause until you reactivate. Nothing gets deleted. Come back anytime and everything's still there.
I'm not tech savvy enough to manage a profile.
You don't have to be. Claiming is a form — name, email, done. After that we walk you through everything. If you're on Pro, we help set up the page for you. You tell us about your gym, we handle the rest.
