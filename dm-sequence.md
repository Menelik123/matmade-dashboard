## Message 2 — No Reply Follow-Up (Day 3)

> Hey, just following up on this — didn't want it to get buried.
>
> Your MatMade page is getting traffic. People searching for BJJ in [City] are landing on it right now. Just want to make sure what they see is accurate and that you're the one controlling it.
>
> Claim link is free: [matmade.com/claim or direct URL]

## Message 3 — The Story Hook (Day 5 · After they respond or claim)

> Hey [Gym Name] — following up on your MatMade page.
>
> We're building out our Transformation Story series for the MatMade Instagram (@matmade_show) and we're starting in [State] this week.
>
> We're looking for gyms with real stories — students whose lives BJJ changed. Depression, anxiety, veterans finding their footing, people who found community after a loss — that kind of story.
>
> Does your gym have someone like that? Kenny Kim is personally selecting the gyms we feature.
>
> No cost at all — just a 15-minute conversation with the student. Their story goes on our Instagram and lives permanently on your MatMade page.

## Message 4 — City Feature Close (Day 7 · Claimed gyms only)

> Hey [Gym Name] — your gym is verified on MatMade now.
>
> One more thing before we move on to the next city.
>
> We're opening the Featured Gym spot for [City] this week. Here's what that means — your gym goes to the TOP of the [City] page. First thing anyone sees when they search BJJ in [City].
>

>
> It's $149/month. If you want it reply and I'll hold the spot for 24 hours. If not I'll move to the next gym on the list.
>
> Your call.

> Welcome to the verified side! Your listing is now claimed — nice.
>
> Quick heads up: Premium unlocks your hero image, video, class schedule, booking link, and social handles on the page — basically turns it into a real marketing asset instead of just a placeholder.
